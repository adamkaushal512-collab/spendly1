Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep your financial information safe.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

01Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run Spendly:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and request logs, used to keep the service running and secure.

We do not connect to your bank accounts or collect card details.

02How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and category breakdowns.
  • Fix problems, prevent misuse, and improve the service.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

03Data Storage

Your data is stored in Spendly's database and kept only for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable steps to protect it, including hashing passwords and limiting access.

You can delete individual expenses at any time. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your personal and expense data, except where we are required by law to keep it.

04Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers, such as hosting services and font providers (for example, Google Fonts), to deliver the app. These providers may receive basic technical information like your IP address when your browser loads their resources.

We do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

05Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access or delete your data, please contact the Spendly team. You can also read our Terms and Conditions.

In short: we collect only what Spendly needs, we never sell your data, and you can delete it whenever you like.
